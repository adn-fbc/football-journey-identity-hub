# Phase 0 Data Classification

**Status:** Proposed controls for review, not a legal classification or compliance claim. Retention and disclosure rules marked `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED` must be approved before real data is loaded. Apply the highest applicable classification to a combined record or projection.

## Approved development/testing restriction

Under DECISION-002 (`APPROVED`), adult test accounts may be used for account/authentication testing, and synthetic/dummy player data may be used for player profiles and football records. Real children's personal data is **PROHIBITED** during initial development and testing: do not import, collect, or use it; do not enable real child-profile sharing or child scouting discovery; and do not expose it to scouts, clubs, academies, or other organizations. This does not authorize implementation of a real child-data system and does not state a legal-compliance conclusion.

| Data set | Development/testing status | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Adult authentication test accounts | **ALLOWED** | Use only for account/authentication testing in a non-production environment. Do not treat account testing as approval to process child data. |
| Synthetic/dummy player profiles and football records | **ALLOWED** | Fictional data only; clearly identify fixtures/test data and keep it isolated from production. |
| Real children's personal or football data | **PROHIBITED** | No import, collection, use, sharing, scouting discovery, or organizational exposure during initial development/testing. |

Real child data remains out of scope until safeguards have been designed, implemented, independently reviewed, and tested, with every release gate in [phase-0-readiness-checklist.md](phase-0-readiness-checklist.md) approved. No legal age threshold is inferred.

## Access rules that apply across classes

1. Store the minimum fields needed for a defined product purpose. Classification attaches to fields/records and to derived projections, not just tables.
2. Missing classification is treated as the more restrictive applicable class; unknown data is not assumed public.
3. Access evaluates authenticated actor, active assignment, exact record scope, action, purpose, current consent where required, and classification.
4. Client-selected role/tenant/person IDs do not authorize access. API/server and database paths independently enforce scope.
5. Do not place private child/guardian data in logs, error payloads, global search indexes, public URLs, client bundles, analytics tags, or AI context without an approved purpose and explicit controls.
6. Revocation stops future access and should invalidate scoped caches/links. It cannot recall data already lawfully viewed or exported; export workflows must communicate this limitation.
7. RLS policies and service policies must agree. Use deny-by-default for exposed schemas, explicit tenant/subject predicates, and tests for reads and all writes. A broad authenticated-role grant is not sufficient authorization.
8. File/document objects inherit at least the classification of their contents; use private storage and authorization-checked, short-lived delivery links if storage is approved.
