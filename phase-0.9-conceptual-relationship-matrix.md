# Phase 0.9 — Conceptual Relationship Matrix

**Status:** Provider-neutral candidate relationships for owner/domain review. This matrix is not an ERD or physical data model. It does not specify tables, keys, columns, constraints, or final cardinalities. `Known` means only that the domain meaning is supported by an approved invariant; it does not imply a physical relationship or implementation.

| Source concept | Target concept | Relationship meaning | Cardinality status | Direction / ownership | Lifecycle implications | Authorization implications | Related decisions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Login identity / Auth subject | Account | A provider-authenticated subject may be associated with an application principal. | Unresolved | Provider controls credentials; application account association is not provider selection. | Linking, recovery, suspension, and unlinking need policy. | Verified authentication proves session control only; it is not person/player authorization. | P-01–P-05, P-11 |
| Account | Person | Candidate account-person association. | Unresolved | No mandatory one-to-one or one-to-many direction is selected. | Association, verification, correction, and recovery are undecided. | A link cannot be treated as final identity resolution until its verification/policy passes. | P-05, P-11, D-33 |
| Person | Player | Candidate identity resolution; a person does not automatically become a player. | Unresolved | No automatic parent/child ownership or mandatory one-to-one relation. | Player creation, duplicate resolution, merge, split, and corrections must retain historical references. | Person role or account presence does not authorize player access. | P-05, P-11, D-33 |
| Player | National Football ID | One stable, player-owned identifier refers to the player identity. | Known semantic invariant; format/issuance unresolved | ID belongs to Player Identity, never an organization; no identifier format or generator is chosen. | Stability across affiliation changes; collision, correction, merge, compromise, and retirement remain open. | Knowing the ID proves no authentication or authorization. | DECISION-001, D-24, D-33 |
| Organization | Organization Membership | A Person may participate in an Organization under scoped roles. | Candidate; cardinality unresolved | Membership is scoped to an organization; no inherited global authority. | Claim/invitation, verification, role changes, revocation, and owner transfer are open. | Membership must not grant player consent, unrelated history, or Platform Admin access. | P-06, P-07, D-25, D-26 |

| Organization | Team | Team operates in an organization context. | Candidate; exact cardinality unresolved | Do not infer governance parentage or ownership beyond the operational context. | Team creation, transfer, closure, and season changes are unresolved. | Organization scope does not automatically grant full player history. | P-07, D-25, D-27 |
| Player | Player Affiliation | Dated relationship between Player identity and Organization, preserving history. | Known semantic invariant; overlap constraints unresolved | Organization may steward its own operational facts; it does not own Player identity. | Concurrent valid affiliations are allowed by ADR-10; temporal/correction rules remain open. | Affiliation is not blanket access or historical sharing. | DECISION-001, ADR-10, P-08–P-10 |

| Competition | Tournament | Tournament may be a Competition form or a distinct conceptual grouping. | Unresolved | No separate entity/table or inheritance direction is required. | Bracket/phase and competition lifecycle remain conceptual. | Organizer scope must be limited to approved operations. | D-28, D-34 |

| Competition Registration | Eligibility | Registration and eligibility may be evaluated together but are distinct facts. | Unresolved | No direction or ownership of evaluation is selected. | Eligibility conditions, windows, decision, appeal, and re-evaluation remain open. | Registration does not prove eligibility; eligibility does not grant access or prove participation. | D-28, D-36 |

| Match | Match Participation | A player may be recorded as having participated in a Match. | Candidate; cardinality unresolved | Match/Participation domain is a candidate source; official authority is not fully defined. | Lineup, actual participation, corrections, disputes, and verification need rules. | Registration or eligibility is not proof of actual participation. | D-29, D-36 |
| Person | Guardian Relationship | A person may make a guardian relationship claim about a Player. | Relationship meaning established; cardinality/verification unresolved | No legal authority is inferred from the relationship. | Claim, verification, dispute, expiry, and revocation need policy. | Verification alone grants no permission or authority. | ADR-11, P-12, P-13 |
| Guardian Relationship | Guardian Authority | A verified relationship may be an input to, but is not equivalent to, authority. | Unresolved | Authority source and evidence must be decided by accountable owners. | Establishment, expiry, conflict, suspension, and revocation are open. | Must be assessed for subject, action, scope, purpose, and policy. | ADR-11, P-12–P-15 |

| Business operations / authorization decisions | Audit Event | Audit records the minimum security/accountability evidence for an operation. | Candidate; coverage/cardinality unresolved | Restricted audit stewardship; business facts remain owned by their domains. | Retention, access, integrity, legal hold, and deletion remain policy questions. | Audit access is separately controlled and never bypasses source authorization. | P-18, D-32 |

## Interpretation limits

- Arrows and association language above describe candidate meaning only; they do not prescribe data flow, physical direction, cardinality, ownership, or a table boundary.
- `Known semantic invariant` does not mean an approved implementation or schema.
- All unresolved relationships must remain visibly unresolved through logical-model review; do not convert this matrix into a physical ERD without owner/domain approval.
