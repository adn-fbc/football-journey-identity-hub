**Status:** Carry-forward for owner/domain review. This document proposes no decision, approval, provider, schema, lifecycle, or implementation authorization. ## Approved decisions and ADRs remain unchanged

- **DECISION-001:** Player-owned stable National Football ID; organization-independent; never reassigned because of affiliation or transfer. Planning invariant only, not ID format/implementation approval.
- **DECISION-002:** Adult test accounts for authentication testing and synthetic/dummy player data only during initial development/testing; real-child data/sharing/scouting prohibited until gates pass.
- **DECISION-003:** Inspect existing provider context before provider selection; inspection approval does not authorize provider changes.
- **ADR-09–ADR-12:** Assessment authorship/review/revisions; global player identity and dated affiliations; independent verified guardian relationship and scoped consent; controlled cross-organization continuity/sharing. Their accepted meaning must not be changed without owner approval.

## Child-safety and minimization gate

Until guardian verification, authority, scoped consent, child-safety/safeguarding controls, auditability, and independent review are designed and tested, use adult test accounts only for authentication tests and synthetic/dummy Player data only. Do not introduce real child data/media, activate real-child scouting/search/profile sharing, or test identities of real children. Do not infer consent from public visibility. Review fixtures, screenshots, seeds, demo accounts, logs, analytics, exports, test reports, media URLs, and error messages; the status of uninspected channels remains `NOT VERIFIED`.
